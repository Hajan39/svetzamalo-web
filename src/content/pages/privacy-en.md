## Data We Collect

- **Email address**: to send the ebook, newsletter, or order confirmation
- **Full name**: to process an order
- **Delivery address or chosen pickup point**: for orders of physical goods only, to deliver them
- **Phone number**: for orders of physical goods only, optional, for the carrier's delivery notice

## Why We Process Data

- Delivery of ordered or free ebook
- Delivery of ordered physical goods
- Sending order confirmation, payment instructions and dispatch notice
- Newsletter (with consent only)
- Payment processing via Comgate, a.s. payment gateway

The legal basis for processing order data is performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, and consent under (a) for the newsletter. Retaining order data for the period stated below rests on a legal obligation under (c).

## Payment Gateway Comgate

Card and online payments are processed by **Comgate, a.s.**, Company ID: 279 24 505, registered at Gočárova třída 1754/48b, Hradec Králové, Czech Republic. Your payment data is transmitted exclusively to Comgate, a.s. and is not stored on our servers.

## Passing Data to the Carrier

For an order of physical goods we pass the carrier your name, the delivery address or chosen pickup point and, where you give them, your phone number and email. The parcel cannot be delivered without this. The carrier processes that data as a separate controller for the purpose of transport.

## Data Retention

Email addresses and names are retained for the period necessary to fulfil the above purposes. Order data, the delivery address included, is retained for the period required by accounting and tax law. Data is not sold or shared with third parties, except for carriers and providers of essential technical services.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Send requests to: info@svetzamalo.cz.

## Analytics and Cookies

The site does not use analytics tools or advertising cookies in its default configuration. If analytics are enabled, users will be notified via a cookie banner.
