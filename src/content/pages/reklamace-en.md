The e-shop sells two kinds of goods and different rules apply to each. Digital products are the ebooks; physical goods are the prints made from photographs, the accessories and the printed book.

## Digital Products (Ebook)

Under Section 1837(l) of the Czech Civil Code (Act No. 89/2012 Coll.), consumers may not withdraw from a contract for digital content delivered electronically once delivery has commenced with the consumer's explicit consent before the withdrawal period expires.

If you receive a defective or unreadable file, notify us at info@svetzamalo.cz within 6 months of purchase. We will send a corrected or replacement file.

## Physical Goods: Withdrawal Within 14 Days

As a consumer you have the right under Section 1829 of the Civil Code to withdraw from the contract **within 14 days of receiving the goods**, without giving a reason. The deadline is met if you send us the withdrawal on its last day at the latest.

Send your withdrawal to info@svetzamalo.cz, quoting the order number or variable symbol. Then return the goods to the operator's address given above, no later than 14 days after withdrawing.

- The cost of returning the goods is yours.
- Return the goods undamaged and complete. You are liable for any reduction in their value caused by handling them beyond what is needed to establish their nature and properties.
- We refund within 14 days of the withdrawal, to the same payment method unless we agree otherwise. The refund includes the postage you paid when buying, up to the cost of the cheapest delivery option offered. We may hold the refund until the goods reach us, or until you show that you have sent them.

### When withdrawal does not apply

Under Section 1837(d) of the Civil Code, the right of withdrawal does not cover goods made to your specification or personalised for you. Where such goods are offered, this is stated in the product description before you order.

## Physical Goods: Defects

As a consumer you may report a defect in the goods **within two years of receiving them**, under Section 2165 of the Civil Code. A defect appearing within the first year is presumed to have been there on delivery.

File a claim by email at info@svetzamalo.cz. Describe the defect and attach a photograph, which speeds things up. Then send the goods to the operator's address.

Depending on the nature of the defect you are entitled to have it removed by replacement or repair, or to a reasonable discount or withdrawal from the contract.

We reimburse the costs reasonably incurred in making a justified claim, postage included.

### Damage in transit

If a parcel is visibly damaged on delivery, we recommend filing a damage report with the carrier and letting us know at info@svetzamalo.cz. It is not a condition of the claim, but it makes it easier to settle.

## Order Cancellation (Bank Transfer)

If you placed an order via bank transfer and payment has not yet been credited, you may cancel the order by emailing info@svetzamalo.cz. Please include your order number or variable symbol.

## Refunds

If a product was not delivered due to a technical error or any fault on the operator's side, you are entitled to a full refund. Submit your request to info@svetzamalo.cz with your order number. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 14 days of claim approval.

## Complaint Resolution Time

Complaints will be resolved without undue delay, and no later than 30 days from the date of filing.

## Out-of-Court Dispute Resolution

Czech consumers may use the **Czech Trade Inspection Authority (ČOI)** for out-of-court dispute resolution: Štěpánská 567/15, 120 00 Prague 2, www.coi.cz.
