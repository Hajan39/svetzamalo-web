## Delivery Method

The e-shop sells two kinds of goods: **digital products** (an ebook in PDF or EPUB format) and **physical goods** (prints made from photographs, accessories and the printed book). Delivery differs for each.

### Digital products

The ebook is sent electronically to the email address provided during checkout:

- **Card or bank button payment:** automatically after payment confirmation, usually within a few minutes
- **Bank transfer:** within 3 business days of payment being credited

### Physical goods

Goods are dispatched once payment is credited, usually within 3 business days. We email you when the parcel is handed to the carrier, including the tracking number where the carrier issues one.

We ship within the Czech Republic. For delivery elsewhere, write to info@svetzamalo.cz and we will arrange it individually.

The delivery options on offer and their current price are always shown in the cart before you place the order. Depending on the shop settings these are some of:

- **Zásilkovna, pickup point:** delivery to a chosen pickup point
- **Zásilkovna, to your address:** delivery to the address you give
- **Česká pošta:** delivery to the address you give
- **Personal pickup:** by arrangement, free of charge

The carrier's own delivery time starts when the parcel is handed over and is usually 1 to 2 business days. We do not guarantee it, because it is not ours to control.

### Postage

The postage for each delivery option is shown in the cart and forms part of the order total. Above the amount stated in the cart, postage is free; the goods total before postage is what counts.

### Parcels not collected

If you do not collect the parcel and it comes back to us, we will write to the email on the order and agree on re-sending it or refunding you. Re-sending is paid by the buyer, at the cost of postage.

## Payment Methods

Payments are processed by the [Comgate, a.s.](https://www.comgate.eu/cs/platebni-brana) payment gateway.

### Online Card Payment

When paying by card, you will be redirected to the secure ComGate payment gateway. After entering your card details, the payment is verified and processed immediately. Payment data is transmitted securely and is not stored on our servers.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and Maestro cards.

### Bank Payment Buttons

When paying via a bank payment button, you will be redirected directly to your online banking, where you confirm the payment. Funds are sent instantly. This method is supported by major Czech banks (ČSOB, Komerční banka, Raiffeisenbank, Moneta, and others).

### Bank Transfer

After placing your order you will receive payment instructions by email (account number, variable symbol, amount). Please complete the transfer within 5 business days.
